# Competitive Landscape

This is an initial research agenda, not a completed market study. Current capabilities and policies should be verified before making competitive claims.

## Cashback Monitor

Study:

- Breadth of portal comparisons
- Store-level history
- Update cadence and freshness disclosure
- Strengths in rewards discovery
- Limitations in product and retailer guidance

## Evreward

Study:

- Combination of coupons, bonuses, and portal rates
- Historical “recent high” presentation
- Data freshness and coverage

## Rakuten

Study:

- Consumer simplicity
- Retailer coverage
- Cash versus points options where applicable
- Browser-extension experience

## Capital One Shopping and Honey

Study:

- Coupon automation
- Price comparison
- Browser-extension behavior
- Privacy and account requirements

## Google Shopping

Study:

- Product and seller breadth
- Price and availability comparison
- Weaknesses in nuanced retailer quality and rewards

## Wirecutter and editorial review sites

Study:

- Product-trust model
- Testing methodology
- Affiliate incentives
- Limited purchase-path rewards intelligence

## Slickdeals and deal communities

Study:

- Community validation
- Deal velocity
- Noise and deal-quality variability

## Keepa and CamelCamelCamel

Study:

- Historical price visualization
- Alerting
- Amazon-centric limitations

## Proxy and forwarding services

Study examples such as Buyee, ZenMarket, Tenso, Blackship, and similar services. Verify current service models, fees, restrictions, and reputation before recommendations.

## Product differentiation hypothesis

Shopping Rewards Intelligence can differentiate by combining:

- Product suitability
- Retailer intelligence
- Immediate rewards overlays
- International acquisition support
- Transparent uncertainty
- Modular conversational depth
